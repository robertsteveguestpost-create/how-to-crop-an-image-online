How to Crop an Image Online

Cropping is the simplest edit that makes the biggest difference. Removing empty space, centring the subject and matching the shape a platform expects can turn an average photo into one that looks deliberate.

Crop vs resize

Cropping cuts away part of the picture to change what is in the frame and its shape. Resizing keeps everything in the frame but changes the pixel dimensions. Most jobs need both: crop to the right shape first, then resize to the exact size required.

  1. Open a free tool such as the Toolzzz Crop Image tool.
  2. Upload your picture.
  3. Pick a preset ratio or drag the box freely.
  4. Position the subject, then download the cropped image.

Composition tips

  • Leave a little space above the head in portraits.
  • Place the main subject on a third of the frame rather than dead centre for a more natural look.
  • Crop out distracting edges such as half-visible people or bright objects.
  • Do not crop so tight that the image has to be enlarged a lot afterwards, or it will look blurry.
